Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Minty Box ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game RPS (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, processed, and shared when you use the App.

For details about specific activities, see Advertising, Gameplay Analytics, and Account and Data Deletion below.

1. Information We Process

The App does not require users to provide payment card information directly to us.

Depending on the features you use, the App and our service providers may process the following information:

  • Device and technical information, such as device identifiers, IP address, operating system information, app version, language, and diagnostic information.
  • Advertising information, including advertising identifiers and interactions with advertisements.
  • Consent and privacy choices made through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • Gameplay information, such as scores, ranking information, game progress, friend and multiplayer activity, and related identifiers necessary to operate game features.
  • Profile information, such as a service-generated anonymous account identifier, generated or chosen nickname, and country code estimated from device locale or mobile network information or chosen by you.
  • When gameplay analytics are enabled, app activity, play-attempt identifiers, session or gameplay events, and app or device identifiers used by Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Purchase-related information, such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts, and entitlement status used to verify and deliver in-app purchases.

We do not directly collect or store your full payment card details.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising identifiers, and ad interaction information for purposes including:

  • delivering advertisements;
  • measuring advertising performance;
  • preventing fraud and abuse;
  • providing personalized advertising where permitted and consented to.

Where required by applicable law, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage your advertising and privacy choices.

Users in applicable regions may be able to review or change their privacy choices through the Privacy Options feature available in the App.

For more information about Google's practices, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed by the platform through which you obtained the App, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

We may receive and process transaction identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts, product identifiers, and purchase status in order to:

  • verify purchases;
  • provide purchased virtual items or entitlements;
  • prevent duplicate fulfillment and fraud;
  • restore eligible purchases.

We do not receive your complete credit card or other payment account information.

4. Game Services and Leaderboards

The App may process gameplay-related information necessary to provide features such as rankings, scores, purchases, and player entitlements.

We use backend infrastructure, including Supabase, to support certain online game features and securely store related service data.

The information stored may include game identifiers, score or ranking records, transaction records, entitlement information, and technical data necessary to operate these services.

When you first use online features, RPS may create an anonymous game account and a generated nickname. A service-generated user identifier is used to manage your profile and online features. The App may estimate a country code from your device's locale or available mobile network country information and send it to Supabase as part of your profile, including before you manually confirm or change the country. This is not precise GPS location tracking.

Depending on the features you use, online data may also include friend relationships, battle invitations, match status and results, and statistics. Other players may see the nicknames, country labels, scores or results that relevant game features make visible to them.

5. Gameplay Analytics

RPS includes Google Analytics for Firebase to help us understand gameplay, onboarding, app quality, and game balance. Analytics collection depends on the version of the App, its configuration, and applicable privacy requirements; it may be disabled. When analytics are enabled, Firebase may process an app instance identifier, app and device information, automatic app events, and game-specific events such as play starts and ends, game mode, outcome, duration, valid input count, stage progress, and score. A randomly generated identifier may link events from a single play attempt.

We may analyze these events using Google Analytics for Firebase and export them to Google BigQuery for gameplay and service improvement. Our custom gameplay events are not intended to contain your email address, nickname, friend invitation code, or purchase token. Firebase may collect additional information automatically depending on its settings.

Gameplay analytics and advertising have separate purposes. An advertising choice made through UMP should not be understood as permission for every type of gameplay analytics. Where a choice or other legal basis is required, our data processing must follow that requirement. Details of your rights and requests are provided below.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

The App uses third-party services that may process information under their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform — advertising and consent management
  • Google Play — Android app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — iOS app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Supabase — anonymous game accounts, online services and data storage
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Google BigQuery — gameplay analytics and data analysis, when enabled

These third parties process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Purpose of Processing

We process information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • operate and maintain the App;
  • provide gameplay and online features;
  • provide rankings and game services;
  • verify and fulfill purchases;
  • display and measure advertisements;
  • when enabled, measure gameplay activity to improve onboarding, usability, reliability and game balance;
  • respect advertising and privacy choices;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and technical problems;
  • comply with legal and platform requirements.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing game services, maintaining purchase records, preventing fraud, resolving disputes, and complying with legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service involved. Analytics data, when collected, may be subject to separate Google Analytics and BigQuery retention settings. Stopping future collection does not by itself delete information collected previously.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through the App.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required, appropriate safeguards are used in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

11. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other privacy choices.

Advertising and consent choices may also be managed through the Privacy Options feature in the App where available. Advertising preferences and gameplay analytics collection are distinct. If you have questions about analytics processing or wish to make a privacy request, please contact us below.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the address below.

12. Account and Data Deletion Requests

RPS may create an anonymous game account for its online services. You can request deletion of your RPS game account and the personal data associated with it by emailing Minty Box Support. Please use the subject RPS Account and Data Deletion Request. To help us locate the account, you may include your game nickname and a brief description of your request. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens, or other secret credentials.

We may need to verify your connection to the game account before taking action. Some transaction, security, or legally required records may need to be retained where applicable. We will explain any such limitations when responding to your request. Requests to delete an account and requests to stop future analytics collection are different: one does not automatically complete the other.

13. Children's Privacy

The App will comply with applicable laws and platform requirements relating to children and minors.

Where age-related restrictions or consent requirements apply, advertising and data processing practices may be adjusted as required by law and platform policies.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technologies, legal requirements, or platform requirements change.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
