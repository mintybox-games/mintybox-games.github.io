Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Minty Box ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game RPS (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, processed, and shared when you use the App.

1. Information We Process

The App does not require users to provide payment card information directly to us.

Depending on the features you use, the App and our service providers may process the following information:

  • Device and technical information, such as device identifiers, IP address, operating system information, app version, language, and diagnostic information.
  • Advertising information, including advertising identifiers and interactions with advertisements.
  • Consent and privacy choices made through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • Gameplay information, such as scores, ranking information, game progress, and related identifiers necessary to operate game features.
  • Purchase-related information, such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts, and entitlement status used to verify and deliver in-app purchases.

We do not directly collect or store your full payment card details.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising identifiers, and ad interaction information for purposes including:

  • delivering advertisements;
  • measuring advertising performance;
  • preventing fraud and abuse;
  • providing personalized advertising where permitted and consented to.

Where required by applicable law, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage your advertising and privacy choices.

Users in applicable regions may be able to review or change their privacy choices through the Privacy Options feature available in the App.

For more information about how Google processes data, please review Google's privacy and advertising information.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed by the platform through which you obtained the App, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

We may receive and process transaction identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts, product identifiers, and purchase status in order to:

  • verify purchases;
  • provide purchased virtual items or entitlements;
  • prevent duplicate fulfillment and fraud;
  • restore eligible purchases.

We do not receive your complete credit card or other payment account information.

4. Game Services and Leaderboards

The App may process gameplay-related information necessary to provide features such as rankings, scores, purchases, and player entitlements.

We use backend infrastructure, including Supabase, to support certain online game features and securely store related service data.

The information stored may include game identifiers, score or ranking records, transaction records, entitlement information, and technical data necessary to operate these services.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

The App uses third-party services that may process information under their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform — advertising and consent management
  • Google Play — Android app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — iOS app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Supabase — backend infrastructure and data storage

These third parties process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Purpose of Processing

We process information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • operate and maintain the App;
  • provide gameplay and online features;
  • provide rankings and game services;
  • verify and fulfill purchases;
  • display and measure advertisements;

  • prevent fraud, abuse, and technical problems;
  • comply with legal and platform requirements.
